# FleetOps Enterprise - Vehicle Management Portal

A high-density commercial fleet telematics and asset lifecycle portal designed for logistics supervisors, depot controllers, and enterprise fleet managers.

## 🚛 Core Enterprise Features
- **High-Density Fleet Ledger**: Comprehensive overview of commercial haulers, delivery vans, electric fleets, and executive transport.
- **Maintenance & Workshop Work Orders**: Log workshop visits, authorized vendors, maintenance classification, part replacement details, invoice amounts, and odometer readings.
- **Service Timeline & Inspection Alerts**: Automatically flags vehicles with inspection due or overdue service dates.
- **Dynamic Fleet Reassignment**: Easily reassign drivers, emergency contacts, operating depots, and operational readiness status.
- **Technical Asset Passport**: Inspect complete historical audit trail and workshop repair logs for any vehicle.
- **Fleet Metrics & Financial Reporting**: Real-time KPI summaries of total active units, workshop load, depot reserves, and monthly fleet maintenance expenses.
- **CSV Fleet Registry Export**: Download complete fleet registry with telematics metrics into an RFC-compliant CSV spreadsheet.
- **Persistence & Factory Reset**: Complete state persistence in `localStorage` with built-in reset to seed data.

## 🚀 How to Run
Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
